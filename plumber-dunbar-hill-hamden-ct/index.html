Dunbar Hill is a residential neighborhood in the southwestern part of Hamden. It is served by the Hamden post office, ZIP code 06514. Its boundaries are not formally defined, so the streets that locals use as reference points matter more. They include Dunbar Hill Road, Benham Street, Main Street, Paradise Avenue, Cooper Lane and Wintergreen Avenue.

The name goes back to Giles Dunbar, who built a home and a woolen mill off Building Brook near Main Street. The area became known as Dunbar and later Dunbar Hill for its terrain.

Hills, lots and what changed after the 1930s

In the 1930s A.C. Gilbert assembled about 600 acres here, and he began selling lots in 1937. About 500 acres had been sold by 1960. Dunbar Hill Elementary School opened in 1951 and Wintergreen School in 1964. Some homes in the neighborhood may therefore date from the middle of the twentieth century, though newer houses and renovations are likely mixed in.

Hills are a plumbing topic in their own right. Water pressure, drainage and basement conditions can all change with the slope of a lot. The sections below explain how.

How a hillside can affect plumbing

These are general principles, so your own lot may differ.

Water pressureHouses lower on a slope can see higher pressure than houses near the top. A pressure gauge on a hose bib shows yours. See water line repair and ask about a pressure reducing valve.
Basement waterWater follows the slope, so a basement at the base of a hill can collect runoff after heavy rain. See sump pump repair.
Drain slopeA basement bathroom below the street level of the sewer may need a pump to move waste uphill. See plumbing installation.
Well or public waterSome properties may be on a private well instead of public water, so confirm what serves your street.

A short visit with a pressure gauge answers most of these.

Quick checks you can do yourself

No tools are needed for most of these.

1
Test the pressureScrew an inexpensive gauge onto an outdoor hose bib and read it with no fixtures running.
2
Look at the basement floorStains, white mineral lines or damp corners show where water has been.
3
Check the sump pitIf you have one, make sure it is clear and the float moves freely.
4
Read the water heater labelThe date and capacity help us plan a replacement if one is needed.

What to do first if water is coming into your basement

These steps limit damage and risk until help arrives.

  1. Stay out of standing water if any outlet, appliance or cord is in or near it.
  2. Turn off power to the basement at the panel only if you can reach it from dry ground.
  3. If a pipe is the source, close the main water shutoff.
  4. Move belongings off the floor and call a plumber.

Landmarks and utilities on Dunbar Hill

Local reference points include Dunbar Hill Volunteer Fire Company 8, Dunbar Hill Elementary School, Radio Tower Park on Benham Street and Lake Wintergreen in West Rock Ridge State Park. The Wilbur Cross Parkway crosses the southern part of the neighborhood.

Water in Hamden is supplied by the South Central Connecticut Regional Water Authority, which draws most of its supply from reservoirs. Much of the town's sewer system is operated by the Greater New Haven Water Pollution Control Authority. Call 811 before any digging on your property. Plumbing work in Connecticut follows the 2022 State Building Code, which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC).
