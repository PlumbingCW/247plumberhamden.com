Faucet repair in Hamden, CT covers the kitchen, bath, laundry and outdoor fixtures that leak, drip, run weak or will not shut off. A faucet that drips once a second can waste thousands of gallons over a year, and a leak under the sink can rot a cabinet long before you see it.

A faucet or valve that will not shut off, or a supply line spraying under a sink, is an emergency. We answer 24/7 and dispatch day and night. Close the stop valve under the sink, or the main valve if the stop valve will not turn, and call.

Faucet or valve will not shut off

Water running with no way to stop it can flood a cabinet or a floor in minutes. We take after-hours and weekend calls, and we try to book the earliest same-day visit.

Take these steps while you wait.

  1. Turn the oval or round stop valve under the sink clockwise. If it will not move, do not force it.
  2. Close the main water valve where the line enters the house.
  3. Open a lower faucet to relieve pressure and drain the line.
  4. Dry the cabinet and floor, and photograph any damage.

Why faucets drip and leak

Every faucet has a seal, a washer, a cartridge or a ball that controls the flow. Over time the rubber hardens, minerals build up and the seat wears. The drip starts at the spout, or the leak shows at the handle or the base.

Which part fails depends on the style: compression faucets use washers, while cartridge, ball and ceramic disc faucets each have their own parts. We identify the model, replace the worn parts and test for leaks.

Low flow and weak pressure at one faucet

If only one faucet runs weak, the cause is local. Mineral buildup in the aerator, a clogged cartridge or a partly closed stop valve are the usual suspects. Cleaning or replacing those fixes it.

If every fixture is weak, the issue is in the house supply, and our water line repair page covers service line and pressure problems.

Shower and tub valves

Shower faucets live inside the wall, and a leaking or hard-to-turn valve means the cartridge is worn or the valve body is corroded. Many cartridges can be replaced from the front. If the valve body itself is failing, the repair requires access from behind the wall, often through an adjacent room or a small opening.

A drip from a shower head when it is off, or hot and cold water mixing at the wrong temperature, are both cartridge symptoms.

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet with a failing finish, a discontinued cartridge or repeated leaks may be better replaced. We can install one you choose, and we suggest models from makers who keep parts available for years.

If you are redoing a whole kitchen or bath, see plumbing installation for supply, drain and fixture work.

Water quality in Hamden and faucet wear

Mineral scale on aerators and cartridges is a common reason for slow flow, and it shows up as white or tan crust at the spout. Hamden is in the service area of the South Central Connecticut Regional Water Authority, and you can check its published water quality report for your area.

Soaking an aerator in vinegar clears light buildup, and it is worth doing a couple of times a year.

Standards that affect faucets

Faucet repairs are usually maintenance, but replacement fixtures must meet the plumbing code. Connecticut's 2022 State Building Code is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which expects fixtures to be listed to recognized standards. Check the box for the listing marks, and ask us before buying an unusual fixture.
