New Haven is the large city directly south of Hamden, and several of its neighborhoods share a border with ours. When a sewer line backs up or a pipe bursts in a historic house in ZIP 06511 and the surrounding area, we answer around the clock and try to book the earliest same-day visit.

We are not the only plumber in a dense market, so this page explains how we work in the city's older neighborhoods and what to do while you wait.

Neighborhoods we work in

On the northern side of the city, East Rock, Cedar Hill, Prospect Hill and Newhallville run toward the Hamden line, while Dixwell and Westville lie to the west and northwest. Each has its own mix of housing, from multi-family buildings to single-family homes on small lots.

In neighborhoods with attached or closely spaced buildings, a backup in one unit can trace to a shared line. We ask about the building before we arrive, because the fix may involve a neighbor, a landlord or the city's sewer line connection.

Historic housing and the plumbing behind the plaster

A large share of New Haven's land is in National Register historic districts, which means many buildings date from the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. Plumbing in these buildings has usually been replaced in pieces, so cast iron, copper, galvanized steel and plastic can all appear in one house.

That mix is why we start with a look before quoting. A short camera inspection or a leak detection visit tells us what you actually have, and it often saves a larger repair later.

Sewers and the GNHWPCA

The Greater New Haven Water Pollution Control Authority serves New Haven, Hamden, East Haven and Woodbridge, and it lists an emergency line of 203-466-5260 on its website. Use it to report a problem with the public sewer in the street.

The pipe from your building to the street connection is generally the owner's responsibility. We repair that portion, and permit rules vary, so ask the city before any excavation. Plumbing repairs follow the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC).
