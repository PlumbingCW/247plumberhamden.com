Emergency plumber in Hamden, available 24/7

When a pipe lets go at two in the morning, you need an emergency plumber in Hamden who answers the phone. Our line is open 24/7, every day of the year, and we try to book the earliest same-day visit for every call. We start by helping you stop the water, then we send a licensed plumber and explain the work and the price before we begin.

Most of what we see falls into a handful of problems. A drain backs up and sewage threatens the basement. A tank water heater leaks onto the floor. A pipe splits in a cold exterior wall. A sump pump fails during a storm. Each has its own page, such as emergency drain cleaning, water heater replacement and sump pump repair, written for the moment you are reading it.

Neighborhoods We Serve

Hamden is made up of named neighborhoods that have no official boundaries.
